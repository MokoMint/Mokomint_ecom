About Our Company

Welcome to MokoMint

At MokoMint, we design premium educational wooden puzzles and toys that help young minds grow. We started with a simple goal: to replace plastic screens and low-quality toys with meaningful, hands-on learning tools that children genuinely enjoy playing with.

Every puzzle we make is crafted with care from natural, sustainably-sourced beech and birch wood. We take the safety of your little ones seriously, which is why all our toys feature smooth rounded corners and are finished exclusively with non-toxic, child-safe paints and protective organic oils.

By blending timeless wooden craftsmanship with foundational learning concepts—covering languages, early mathematics, color identification, and motor skills—we ensure that every piece provides hours of healthy, independent exploration for smart toddlers.

100% Safe & Natural Premium natural wood with zero harmful chemical residues or rough splinters. Proudly Made Local Carefully hand-sanded and precision-molded in our dedicated workshop.
